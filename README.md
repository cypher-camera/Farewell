## 🌟 The Experience

1. **Stage 0: Film Leader Countdown**
   - Classic retro film leader countdown (3, 2, 1) with an animated progress bar and audio.
2. **Stage 1: The Theatrical Opening**
   - A 3D beveled wax-sealed envelope on a dark lacquer table under a warm spotlight.
   - Crack the wax seal, slide open the flap, and flip the gleaming golden ticket.
   - Part the heavy velvet stage curtains to glide into the premiere world.
3. **Stage 2: The Scroll World**
   - **Scene A: The Lobby**: Dynamic marquee sign with chasing perimeter bulbs, live countdown timer, and direct Google Maps navigation link.
   - **Scene B: The Red Carpet**: Velvet burgundy carpet, golden stanchions, velvet ropes, paparazzi flashbulbs, and senior memory quotes (*"Echoes of the Corridor"*).
   - **Scene C: The Cast (Celestial Sphere)**: 3D interactive Fibonacci sphere of floating student frames with golden armillary rings and drag-to-orbit controls.
   - **Scene D: The Directors (Gallery Wall)**: Burgundy velvet-panelled gallery wall with ornate gold frames, real teacher portraits, brass nameplates, and dedication lines.
4. **Theatrical Isolation Spotlight**
   - Clicking any student or teacher frame triggers a high-intensity spotlight beam and volumetric golden light cone, spring-lifting the frame forward and revealing the spotlight inspection modal.
5. **Instant RSVP & WhatsApp Confirmation**
   - Reserve attendance with Class, Section, Grade, and Song requests.
   - Generates an individualized Premiere Ticket Pass with seat assignment.
   - Automatically prepares and opens a WhatsApp confirmation addressed to `+91 7986246076`.
   - One-click Google Calendar integration and client-side `.ics` pass download.
